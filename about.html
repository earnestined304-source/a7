Our Story

Made because "silk" shouldn't need air quotes

We're a small, independent studio — not a large manufacturer. Here's the plain version of how we got here.

Where It Started

A "silk" robe that was mostly something else

Silk Robe Vista started with a specific frustration: a robe labeled silk that turned out to be a thin, synthetic-heavy blend, snagging and pilling within weeks of normal wear. We started sourcing genuine mulberry silk by momme weight, checking construction against real daily use before committing to a single design.

Every piece since has been built around that same tested standard.

How We Work

A few habits we hold to on every piece

I.

Silk Checked by Weight

We verify momme weight directly rather than trusting a supplier's label alone.

II.

French Seams, Not Overlocked

A cleaner, more durable seam finish used throughout every piece.

III.

Small Batches, Checked by Hand

Every batch gets a hands-on quality check before it ships.
